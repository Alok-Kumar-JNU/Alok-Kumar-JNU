# Hi there 👋

I'm **Alok Kumar**, a researcher interested in understanding how cities grow, transform, and shape economic and social inequalities.

### 🔭 Research Interests

* 🏙️ **Urban Geography & Urbanization**
* 📊 **Urban Economics & Regional Inequality**
* 🌆 **Urban Systems and City Growth**
* 🧩 **Complexity Theory and Cities**
* 🤖 **Agent-Based Models & Computational Urban Science**
* 🛰️ **GIS, Remote Sensing & Spatial Analysis**
* 📈 **Urban Scaling and Economic Development**

### 🌱 Currently Learning

I'm exploring new computational and spatial methods to study cities, including:

* Geospatial data science
* Machine learning and embeddings
* Agent-based modelling
* Complexity approaches to urban systems
* Spatial and network analysis

### 👯 Collaboration

I'm interested in collaborating on research related to:

* Urbanization and urban growth
* Indian cities and urban systems
* Small and medium-sized towns
* Urban segregation and inequality
* Regional development
* Computational approaches to geography and economics

### 🤔 What I'm Trying to Understand

> **How do cities grow, and why do different urban systems produce such different economic and social outcomes?**

I am particularly interested in the interface between **Geography and Economics**, and in developing empirical approaches to understand urbanization in India.

### 🎓 Research

**PhD Research:**
*Dynamics of Urbanization in India and its Economic Dimensions*

My broader research examines urban growth, urban systems, economic development, and regional inequality in India.

### 📚 Interests Beyond Research
